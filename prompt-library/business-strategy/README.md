# Business & Strategy

Business models, validation, strategic choices and commercial logic.

## Prompts

- [Business Model Review](business-model-review.md) — Map customer, problem, value, channels, revenue, activities, partners and costs. Test consistency, margins, bottlenecks and assumptions; recommend evidence and three high-leverage changes.
- [Market Validation Plan](market-validation.md) — Identify riskiest assumptions and design low-cost tests with target participants, neutral questions, observable behaviour and pass/fail criteria. Prefer behavioural evidence over compliments.
- [Strategic Options Memo](strategy-options.md) — Frame decision, objective and constraints; compare distinct options on fit, upside, cost, risk, reversibility and capability. Recommend one and state what evidence could change the recommendation.
- [Unit Economics Review](unit-economics.md) — Calculate unit revenue, variable costs, contribution, margins, CAC, payback and break-even from supplied figures. Show formulas, flag missing inputs and label scenarios; never fabricate numbers.

## Use standard
Provide context and source material, review outputs before acting, and keep evidence separate from assumptions. All prompts remain Draft until realistic tests and limitations are recorded.
