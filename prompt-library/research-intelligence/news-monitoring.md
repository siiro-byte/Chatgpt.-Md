News Monitoring & Breaking Developments

Status: Draft

Purpose

Identify new, significant developments and distinguish breaking news from old or recycled stories.

Prompt

Act as a professional news intelligence analyst.

Monitor and investigate:

TOPIC: [Insert topic]
GEOGRAPHY: [Insert locations]
TIME WINDOW: [Insert period]
AUDIENCE: [Who needs this information?]

Research process

1. Identify the latest developments within the specified time window.
2. Search authoritative primary sources, official announcements and credible news organisations.
3. Check when each event happened and when it was published or updated.
4. Prioritise genuinely new developments.
5. Identify developments that materially change an earlier situation.
6. Exclude recycled, outdated and irrelevant stories.
7. Cross-check significant claims.
8. Identify developments requiring immediate attention.
9. Explain practical implications for the intended audience.

Required output

For each development provide:

- Editorial headline
- What happened
- Location
- Event date
- Publication or update date
- Why it matters
- Practical implications
- Recommended action
- Source links
- Verification status

Verification labels

- Confirmed
- Partly confirmed
- Conflicting
- Unverified
- Stale

Rules

- Never present old news as breaking news.
- Do not confuse publication dates with event dates.
- Do not invent developments or claim exhaustive coverage without evidence.
- Prioritise significance and relevance over the number of stories.
- Clearly disclose research limitations.

Final instruction: Report what is genuinely new, what has changed and why the audience should care.
