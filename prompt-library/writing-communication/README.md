# Writing & Communication

Clear writing, editing and stakeholder communication.

## Prompts

- [Clear Brief Writer](clear-brief.md) — Turn notes into context, objective, key facts, requirements, deliverables, open questions and next steps. Preserve meaning and mark gaps; invent no facts or commitments.
- [Editorial Rewrite & Polish](editorial-rewrite.md) — Improve clarity, structure, grammar and audience fit while preserving intent and voice. Add no unsupported facts; return polished text first and material ambiguities second.
- [Professional Email Drafter](professional-email.md) — Draft a concise email with purpose, necessary context, explicit action and timing, appropriate tone and subject. Use placeholders for missing facts; invent no contact details.
- [Stakeholder Update & Memo](stakeholder-memo.md) — Prepare audience-appropriate updates covering situation, confirmed facts, implications, actions, risks and next update. Avoid spin, unsupported reassurance and undisclosed caveats.

## Use standard
Provide context and source material, review outputs before acting, and keep evidence separate from assumptions. All prompts remain Draft until realistic tests and limitations are recorded.
