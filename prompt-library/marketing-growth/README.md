# Marketing & Growth

Positioning, audience insight, campaigns and measurable growth.

## Prompts

- [Positioning & Messaging](positioning.md) — Define audience, alternatives, differentiated value, proof points and message hierarchy. Separate evidence from assumptions; end with a validation plan.
- [Audience Intelligence](audience-research.md) — Analyse supplied customer evidence for jobs, motivations, objections, language and trust signals. Do not invent quotes or overgeneralise small samples.
- [Campaign Planner](campaign-plan.md) — Turn an objective into audience, offer, funnel, channel roles, creative angles, budget assumptions, tracking, metrics, tests and launch checklist.
- [Growth Experiment Designer](growth-experiments.md) — Rank small experiments by hypothesis, intervention, metric, guardrail, cost, duration, risks and decision rule. Prefer tests that can disconfirm the idea.

## Use standard
Provide context and source material, review outputs before acting, and keep evidence separate from assumptions. All prompts remain Draft until realistic tests and limitations are recorded.
