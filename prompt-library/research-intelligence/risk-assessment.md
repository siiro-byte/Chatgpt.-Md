Risk Assessment & Early Warning Intelligence

Status: Draft

Purpose

Identify, evaluate and monitor risks before they become serious problems.

Prompt

Act as a senior risk analyst and early-warning intelligence specialist.

Assess the following:

SUBJECT: [Insert business, destination, project or situation]

GEOGRAPHY: [Insert relevant location]

TIME HORIZON: [Immediate, short-term or long-term]

OBJECTIVE: [What risks need to be identified?]

Assessment process

1. Establish the current situation using reliable evidence.
2. Identify existing and emerging threats.
3. Examine the causes and possible consequences of each risk.
4. Assess likelihood and potential impact.
5. Identify early-warning indicators.
6. Examine existing controls and vulnerabilities.
7. Identify possible scenarios and escalation pathways.
8. Recommend mitigation and contingency measures.
9. Identify information gaps requiring further investigation.
10. Define what developments should trigger reassessment.

Required output

- Executive risk summary
- Current situation
- Risk register
- Likelihood and impact ratings
- Early-warning indicators
- Potential scenarios
- Vulnerabilities
- Mitigation measures
- Contingency actions
- Monitoring priorities
- Evidence and source links
- Confidence and limitations

Rating framework

Likelihood: Low / Medium / High

Impact: Low / Medium / High

Confidence: Low / Medium / High

Explain the reasoning behind every rating.

Rules

- Separate verified facts from forecasts.
- Never present a possibility as a certainty.
- Use current and authoritative sources.
- Do not invent warning indicators or statistics.
- Identify the date and scope of every important assessment.
- Update conclusions when evidence changes.

Final instruction: Identify what could happen, how serious it would be, what signals to watch and what action should follow.
