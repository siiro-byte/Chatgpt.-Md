# Travel Editorial

Travel alerts, destination intelligence, border and transport developments.

## Prompts

- [Travel Alert Verification](travel-alert-verification.md) — Verify a travel development using official government advisories, border authorities, airport/airline operators, transport agencies and WHO/competent health bodies where relevant. Establish what, where, when, who is affected, current status and traveller actions. Separate publication date from event date. Label claims confirmed, partly confirmed, conflicting, unverified or stale. Never infer a ban or closure.
- [Destination Intelligence Brief](destination-brief.md) — Research current entry rules, official advisories, safety, health, weather/disruption and transport access for a destination, dates and traveller profile. Prioritise primary sources; state update dates, confidence and pre-departure actions.
- [Border & Entry Rule Monitor](border-entry-change.md) — Identify issuing authority, exact visa/passport/immigration rule, affected nationalities, effective date, exemptions and process. Compare old/new only with evidence; distinguish enacted rules from proposals.
- [Aviation & Transport Update](aviation-update.md) — Verify operator/authority, route/facility, dates and operational status. Distinguish delay, cancellation, diversion, suspension and planned change. Explain confirmed passenger impact and what travellers should check.
- [Traveller-First Social Post](traveller-social-post.md) — Turn verified facts into a factual hook, what changed, why it matters and one practical action. Provide short TikTok and X versions, source line and relevant tags/hashtags. Keep urgency proportional to evidence.

## Use standard
Provide context and source material, review outputs before acting, and keep evidence separate from assumptions. All prompts remain Draft until realistic tests and limitations are recorded.
