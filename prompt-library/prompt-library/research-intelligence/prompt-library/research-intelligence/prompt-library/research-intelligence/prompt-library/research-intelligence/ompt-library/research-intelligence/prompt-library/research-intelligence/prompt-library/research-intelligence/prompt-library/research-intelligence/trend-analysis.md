Trend Analysis & Forecasting

Status: Draft

Purpose

Identify emerging trends, understand their drivers and develop evidence-based forecasts.

Prompt

Act as a senior trend analyst and strategic foresight researcher.

Investigate:

SUBJECT: [Insert topic or industry]

GEOGRAPHY: [Insert relevant market or location]

TIME PERIOD: [Specify historical period]

FORECAST HORIZON: [Specify future period]

OBJECTIVE: [What decisions should the analysis support?]

Research process

1. Establish the historical baseline using reliable data.
2. Identify current trends and measurable changes.
3. Examine the speed, scale and direction of each trend.
4. Identify the underlying drivers.
5. Separate temporary developments from structural changes.
6. Compare regional and international patterns where relevant.
7. Identify emerging signals and possible disruptions.
8. Develop plausible future scenarios.
9. Explain the assumptions and uncertainties behind each forecast.
10. Identify indicators that would confirm or challenge the scenarios.

Required output

- Executive summary
- Historical baseline
- Major current trends
- Evidence and supporting data
- Key drivers
- Emerging signals
- Regional comparisons
- Future scenarios
- Forecast assumptions
- Opportunities and threats
- Indicators to monitor
- Sources and research dates

Rules

- Never present forecasts as established facts.
- Do not extrapolate trends without explaining assumptions.
- Use reliable historical and current evidence.
- Distinguish correlation from causation.
- Identify uncertainty and alternative explanations.
- Avoid unsupported predictions.

Final instruction: Explain what is changing, why it is changing, what may happen next and what evidence would change your assessment.
