# Image, Video & Audio

Creative briefs, storyboards, scripts and quality review.

## Prompts

- [Image Generation Brief](image-brief.md) — Specify subject, setting, composition, viewpoint, lighting, palette, style, exact text, brand assets, aspect ratio and exclusions. Preserve supplied logos exactly; output a ready-to-use prompt and review checklist.
- [Short-Form Video Storyboard](video-storyboard.md) — Create a timed vertical storyboard with visual, on-screen text, voiceover, transition and purpose per scene. Hook quickly, keep one coherent idea and captions readable; include production checklist.
- [Voiceover Script & Audio Direction](voiceover-script.md) — Write natural spoken narration to target duration, with short sentences, pace, emphasis and pronunciation notes. Keep claims faithful to evidence; include word count.
- [Creative Asset Quality Review](creative-review.md) — Review supplied media against objective, audience, brand, hierarchy, legibility, accessibility, factual accuracy and platform fit. Rank specific fixes; do not claim to inspect unseen details.

## Use standard
Provide context and source material, review outputs before acting, and keep evidence separate from assumptions. All prompts remain Draft until realistic tests and limitations are recorded.
