# Border & Entry Rule Monitor

## Purpose
Determine whether an immigration or border development is a proposal, enacted rule, effective requirement, temporary instruction or unverified report.

## When to use
Use for visa, passport, permit, transit, nationality restriction and border procedure changes.

## Required inputs
- Country and crossing or immigration system
- Reported change or source link
- Traveller nationality/passport and travel date
- Entry, exit or transit purpose

## Instructions
1. Identify the responsible authority and locate its official notice, regulation or legal instrument.
2. Establish status: proposal, bill, enacted law, published rule, announced future change, effective rule, temporary instruction or withdrawn measure. Never describe a proposal as current law.
3. Extract exact scope: affected nationalities, passport types, visa classes, travel purpose, entry/exit/transit, crossing points and exemptions.
4. Record announcement, publication, effective, transition and expiry dates separately.
5. Compare old and new wording only when both versions are available.
6. Verify implementation steps and fees from official guidance; do not invent processing times or costs.
7. Disclose unresolved source conflicts and mark affected points uncertain.
8. Explain the impact for the named traveller and recommend case-specific confirmation with the authority or carrier.

## Expected output
- Change and legal/operational status
- Issuing authority and official instrument
- Affected and exempt travellers
- Old rule vs new rule
- Effective and transition dates
- Documents and process
- Practical traveller impact
- Unresolved issues and official links

## Quality checks
- Proposal not confused with effective law
- Nationality and transit scope explicit
- Effective date verified
- Exemptions checked
- No invented legal interpretation or eligibility guarantee

## Source
Original prompt, informed by regulatory-change monitoring.

## Testing notes
Test input: A social post says a country will introduce a new visa rule next month but gives no legal notice. Observed: the prompt distinguishes proposal/announcement from an effective rule, requires issuing authority, scope and dates, and avoids asserting a current requirement. Result: PASS. Limitation: actual legal status requires primary-source verification.
