# Travel Alerts.ke Research Entry Point

This file is the starting point for research work on Travel Alerts.ke.

## Before researching
1. Retrieve and read the latest `CHATGPT.md` in this repository. It is the master operating guide and contains the full project rules.
2. Apply the relevant specialist sections, including travel safety, regulatory, aviation and transport, traveller services, global destination coverage, and Reddit traveller-signal intelligence.
3. Check recent Travel Alerts.ke research and published updates when available. Do not repeat an existing story unless there is a material new development.

## Research priorities
1. Breaking travel developments with direct traveller impact.
2. Destination safety, security, health, weather, disasters, closures and official advisories.
3. Visas, passports, borders, immigration and entry requirements.
4. Airports, airlines, routes and ground or maritime transport.
5. Tourism, hospitality and destination developments with practical visitor relevance.
6. Global developments that materially affect African travellers or important international routes.

## Geographic coverage
Africa is the primary focus. Maintain balanced coverage across East, West, North, Central and Southern Africa. Include global stories when they have clear relevance to African or international travellers.

## Source discipline
- Use Reddit and social platforms to discover leads and traveller experiences, not as proof.
- Verify significant claims with responsible official authorities and credible independent reporting.
- Record event date separately from publication/update date; confirm whether the issue remains active.
- Distinguish confirmed facts, inference, uncertainty and opinion.
- Use the confidence labels in the master guide: CONFIRMED, PARTLY CONFIRMED, CONFLICTING, UNVERIFIED or STALE.
- Do not invent closures, cancellations, restrictions, route changes or urgency.

## Editorial output
For each candidate, state what happened, where, when, who is affected, why it matters to travellers, what practical action is supported by evidence, the confidence status and the source. Keep posts concise and editorially clear.

## Publication gate
Publish only when the development is current, materially relevant, sufficiently verified and not a duplicate. Otherwise retain it as a monitoring lead or reject it.

**Governing rule:** Freshness and traveller relevance guide discovery. Evidence determines publication. Accuracy and trust take priority over virality.
