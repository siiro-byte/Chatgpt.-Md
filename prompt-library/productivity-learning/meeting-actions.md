# Meeting Notes to Action Plan

## Purpose
Convert meeting transcripts, notes or recordings into an accurate record of key discussion, confirmed decisions, action items, owners, deadlines, dependencies and unresolved questions.

## When to use
Use after project meetings, planning sessions, client calls, workshops, management reviews, interviews or operational handovers.

## Required inputs
- Transcript, notes or recording-derived text
- Meeting date, title and purpose, if known
- Participant names and roles, if known
- Relevant project or decision context
- Desired level of detail and distribution audience

## Instructions
1. Identify the meeting purpose, date and participants only from supplied information. Mark missing metadata rather than guessing.
2. Read the full source and extract topics discussed, evidence shared, proposals, objections, decisions and actions.
3. Separate confirmed decisions from suggestions, options discussed, unresolved questions and informal comments.
4. Capture each action as a concrete task with owner, deadline, dependency and status only where explicitly stated. Use “unassigned” or “not specified” rather than inferring.
5. Preserve exact names, dates, figures, commitments and terminology. Flag ambiguous wording or conflicting statements for confirmation.
6. Group discussion by agenda topic or workstream. Summarise the reasoning and relevant context without reproducing unnecessary transcript detail.
7. Record decisions with the decision-maker or approving group only if known, plus rationale and consequences where stated.
8. Identify blockers, risks, dependencies, issues requiring escalation and items deferred to a later meeting.
9. Create a follow-up list of questions or confirmations needed to make the notes actionable.
10. Produce a concise summary for quick reading and a detailed action register for execution.
11. Do not convert a participant's idea into an agreed commitment unless the source clearly records agreement.
12. If the source is incomplete or unclear, state the limitation and identify which parts require human review.

## Expected output
- Meeting metadata and source limitations
- Executive summary
- Discussion summary by topic
- Confirmed decisions and rationale
- Action register: task, owner, deadline, dependency and status
- Open questions and unresolved issues
- Risks, blockers and escalation items
- Suggested follow-up agenda

## Quality checks
- Decisions and suggestions are clearly separated
- Every action traces to the source
- Owners and deadlines are not invented
- Names, figures and dates are preserved accurately
- Ambiguity and conflicting notes are flagged
- Summary is concise but does not omit material decisions
- Confidential or personal details are not unnecessarily repeated
