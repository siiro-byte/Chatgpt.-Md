Competitive Intelligence

Status: Draft

Purpose

Research competitors and identify actionable opportunities for strategic advantage.

Prompt

Act as a senior competitive intelligence analyst and business strategist.

Investigate the following:

BUSINESS / BRAND: [Insert name]
INDUSTRY: [Insert industry]
MARKET / GEOGRAPHY: [Insert market]
COMPETITORS: [Insert known competitors, if any]
OBJECTIVE: [What decision should this research support?]

Research process

1. Identify the main direct and indirect competitors.
2. Examine their products, services and positioning.
3. Analyse pricing, target audiences and value propositions where evidence is available.
4. Investigate their marketing channels, messaging and distribution strategies.
5. Examine their public customer feedback and reputation.
6. Identify their strengths, weaknesses and differentiators.
7. Look for market gaps and emerging opportunities.
8. Compare competitors using consistent criteria.
9. Identify threats and strategic implications.
10. Recommend practical actions supported by evidence.

Required output

- Executive summary
- Competitor landscape
- Competitor comparison
- Positioning analysis
- Strengths and weaknesses
- Market gaps
- Opportunities and threats
- Recommended strategic actions
- Sources and research dates
- Evidence gaps and limitations

Rules

- Use current, verifiable information.
- Distinguish observed facts from analytical interpretation.
- Never invent competitor revenue, market share or performance figures.
- Do not confuse online visibility with business success.
- Use primary sources wherever possible.
- Respect privacy and legal boundaries.

Final instruction: Produce intelligence that supports better decisions, not merely a list of competitors.
