# Travel Alerts.ke Research Entry Point

This file is the starting point for research work on Travel Alerts.ke.

## Before researching
1. Retrieve and read the latest `CHATGPT.md` in this repository. It is the master operating guide and contains the full project rules.
2. Apply the relevant specialist sections, including travel safety, regulatory, aviation and transport, traveller services, global destination coverage, and Reddit traveller-signal intelligence.
3. Check recent Travel Alerts.ke research and published updates when available. Do not repeat an existing story unless there is a material new development.

## Research priorities
1. Breaking travel developments with direct traveller impact.
2. Destination safety, security, health, weather, disasters, closures and official advisories.
3. Visas, passports, borders, immigration and entry requirements.
4. Airports, airlines, routes and ground or maritime transport.
5. Tourism, hospitality and destination developments with practical visitor relevance.
6. Global developments that materially affect African travellers or important international routes.

## Geographic coverage
Africa is the primary focus. Maintain balanced coverage across East, West, North, Central and Southern Africa. Include global stories only when they materially affect African travellers, important international routes, or a clearly identified traveller decision. Novelty or popularity alone is insufficient.

## Source discipline
- Use Reddit and social platforms to discover leads and traveller experiences, not as proof.
- Verify significant claims with responsible official authorities and credible independent reporting.
- Record event date separately from publication/update date; confirm whether the issue remains active.
- Distinguish confirmed facts, inference, uncertainty and opinion.
- Use East Africa Time (EAT, UTC+3) for daily cut-offs. Prioritise items first published or materially updated in the preceding 24 hours; older items require a verifiable material change in that period.
- Use the confidence labels in the master guide: CONFIRMED, PARTLY CONFIRMED, CONFLICTING, UNVERIFIED or STALE.
- Do not invent closures, cancellations, restrictions, route changes or urgency.
- Separate forecasts and warnings from observed impacts; a forecast alone is not proof of disruption.
- Check whether each issue is active, upcoming, resolved, extended or superseded immediately before drafting.


## Daily research run — final checks

Before preparing the briefing:
1. Search the five African regions and relevant global routes/destinations; check official sources first, then credible media. Use Reddit/social only to discover leads.
2. Compare candidates with previous research and published posts. Repeat a story only for a material, verified change.
3. Recheck the latest status and effective dates immediately before drafting.
4. Keep UNVERIFIED and STALE items out of public alerts; retain them only as monitoring notes where useful.
5. For weather, health and security warnings, name the affected place, travel dates or period, affected travellers and supported action. Do not imply actual disruption where only a forecast or warning exists.
6. If no candidate passes both the traveller-impact and evidence tests, report that no publishable update was verified.

A daily item must show: editorial headline; what changed; precise location; event date and publication/update date; who is affected; concrete traveller consequence; supported action; current status and confidence; direct source link(s). Clearly label an item as an alert, destination feature or hospitality/industry opportunity. Drafts are not published without the user's explicit approval.

## Editorial output
For each candidate, state what happened, where, when, who is affected, why it matters to travellers, what practical action is supported by evidence, the confidence status and the source. Keep posts concise and editorially clear.

## Mandatory traveller-impact publication gate
A candidate qualifies only when there is a specific, current and evidenced consequence for a traveller's decision, journey, destination access, safety, cost or experience. Check borders/documentation; safety and health; passenger transport; destination access/discovery; hotels and hospitality; sustainable tourism; and human–wildlife conflict only where there is a demonstrable visitor or tourism-operation consequence.

Reject or retain as monitoring-only general crime, politics, migration tragedies, public-health statistics, arrests, corporate announcements, conservation news and isolated incidents unless a specific traveller consequence is established. A hotel opening or sustainability announcement is not automatically a travel story. Never infer airport, border, park or destination disruption.

For each item state what changed, exact place and affected dates, who is affected, the specific traveller consequence, supported practical action, original/official evidence, event date, publication/update date, current status, confidence and previous-coverage check. If the consequence or useful action cannot be substantiated, reject it.

Select up to 3–7 strongest items only when they pass; fewer, including none, are acceptable. Never fill a quota. Search all five African regions, but do not force equal regional or category representation. Distinguish alerts from destination features and hospitality/industry updates. Global stories must materially affect African travellers or important international routes. Drafts remain unpublished pending user review and authorisation.

**Governing rule:** Freshness and traveller relevance guide discovery. Evidence determines publication. Accuracy and trust take priority over virality.

## HARD STOP: STORY-LEVEL DEDUPLICATION (2026-11-16)

This gate is mandatory before any daily briefing, radar notification, shortlist, draft, or publication. A new article, new URL, new publication timestamp, social trend, or another outlet repeating a story does NOT make the underlying event new.

1. **Load history first:** retrieve the accessible Content & Source Log, previous 72 hours of research/briefings, published platform posts, and prior radar notifications; check the last 30 days for the same incident or standing advisory. Record which sources were actually accessible. If history is unavailable, mark HISTORY_UNVERIFIED and do not assert an item is new; seek editor review before presenting it as a fresh alert.
2. **Normalize incident identity:** create a stable story key from event type + country + specific airport/border/location/route + responsible authority + original event/effective date. Match semantically, not by headline or URL; aliases, translations, syndication, and different platform posts count as the same incident.
3. **Compare factual delta:** for every matched story, record previous verified state, current verified state, exact newly changed fact, source and timestamp. Only a verified material change qualifies: new closure/reopening, new binding restriction or effective date, newly affected route/population, significant verified escalation/de-escalation, or new actionable official instruction. Mere restatement, unchanged warning, rolling delay statistics, speculation, updated article timestamps, and residual effects already covered are NOT material changes unless the new impact is demonstrably significant and actionable.
4. **Decision:** NEW_VERIFIED (no prior match, original event genuinely new); MATERIAL_UPDATE_VERIFIED (matched story, substantive evidenced delta); DUPLICATE_REJECTED; STALE_REJECTED; MONITOR_ONLY; or HISTORY_UNVERIFIED. Only the first two enter the fresh editorial shortlist. Mark material updates explicitly as 'UPDATE' and explain the delta; never present them as a new incident.
5. **Quota is never a target:** report fewer stories, including zero, rather than substitute repeated or unverified items to fill regional counts. If a previously reported story has no new verified change, omit it from the new-alert list. Keep ongoing context in a separate labelled section only when specifically useful, never as a fresh alert.
6. **Before delivery, second-pass audit:** compare all candidate story keys against each other, against the historical log, and against yesterday's briefing. Reject cross-region duplicate coverage of the same incident unless there is a distinct newly verified traveller consequence. Record per-item story key, last-covered date/platform, delta, evidence, decision and reason.
7. **After delivery:** record researched, rejected, shortlisted, approved and published status separately with event time, source time, first-seen time and publication time in EAT. A research mention is not proof of publication, but it is prior coverage for briefing deduplication. If a durable log cannot be written, explicitly report LOG_WRITE_UNAVAILABLE and do not claim cross-run deduplication is guaranteed.

**Acceptance tests:** yesterday's Bahrain warning repeated today => DUPLICATE_REJECTED; a newly published article about the same US ground stop => DUPLICATE_REJECTED; officially verified airport reopening after yesterday's closure => MATERIAL_UPDATE_VERIFIED; an inaccessible content log => HISTORY_UNVERIFIED; request for nine stories with only four eligible => return four, not nine.

## MANDATORY MULTI-DOMAIN DISCOVERY — ANTI-AVIATION BIAS (2026-11-16)

The Travel Disruption Radar is a specialist aviation/disruption input, NOT the editorial agenda or default source of all daily items. Every general travel research request and daily briefing must independently search all six domains: (1) urgent disruption/emergencies; (2) visas, passports, immigration and borders; (3) destination safety, security, health and visitor access; (4) aviation plus road, rail, ferry and other passenger transport; (5) tourism, hotels, attractions and destination developments; (6) traveller insurance, services, consumer rights and protection. Search each domain across relevant African regions and requested global regions. Record searched/not searched and verified candidate count per domain. Do not fill categories with stale items.

Do not allow aviation stories to dominate merely because they are easier to discover, more frequently reported, or supplied by the hourly radar. Rank by verified novelty, severity, affected travellers and actionable consequence, not source volume. When several equally consequential verified stories compete, prefer diversity of domains and countries; never suppress a uniquely urgent P0/P1 incident to satisfy diversity. Separate destination features and hospitality opportunities from urgent alerts, with accurate labels. Apply the story-level deduplication hard stop to every domain, including visas and standing advisories. If one or more domains have no verified new development, explicitly report the search gap or quiet result rather than substituting airport news. Before delivery audit geographic and topical diversity, and state any justified concentration.

## TREND-LED TRAVELLER SIGNAL DISCOVERY — 16 NOV 2026

The daily editorial agenda must not be built solely from mainstream publisher headlines. In each daily run, use two independent discovery lanes:

**A. Traveller attention / emerging signals:** When actually accessible without paid calls, inspect current public Reddit discussions, Google Trends or publicly visible search interest, public TikTok/X/Instagram posts and discussions, airport/airline passenger notices and local/community reporting. Search relevant travel keywords and route/airport/country terms across West, North, East, Central and Southern Africa and consequential global corridors. Record exact platform, query, URL, observation date/time EAT, visible metric or repeated independent reports, and access limitations. Never claim a topic is 'trending' based only on a major-media headline, one social post, anecdote, syndicated copies, or model intuition. If no reliable trend data are accessible, label TREND_UNVERIFIED; describe the story as 'developing' or 'important' only when supported, not as trending.

**B. Official and independent disruption intelligence:** Independently scan government/immigration, airports/airlines/aviation, health/safety, road/rail/ferry, tourism/destinations and traveller services. This lane catches important stories before they trend. Cross-check every social/viral claim against the responsible authority and credible original reporting. Trend signals are leads, never proof of closures, entry restrictions, danger or passenger impact.

**Cross-check and select:** Merge both lanes by canonical incident key, check prior research and the content log, establish original event date and most recent material change, traveller impact and practical action. Score editorial attention qualitatively as VERIFIED TREND + VERIFIED IMPACT, VERIFIED IMPACT WITHOUT TREND, TREND SIGNAL AWAITING VERIFICATION (monitor only), or REPEAT/STALE (reject). Give strongest placement to genuinely current, actionable and well-substantiated developments with independent evidence of traveller interest; still prioritize verified P0/P1 emergencies even without social traction. Do not turn general popularity, virality, or search spikes into an alert without a concrete traveller consequence.

**Daily output:** Deliver a short list of 3–5 genuinely qualified stories (fewer or zero acceptable). For each show trend evidence and whether verified, primary confirmation, publication/event dates in EAT, prior-coverage delta, why travellers care, recommended action, and approval-only platform suggestion. Separate confirmed alerts from emerging unverified watchlist items. Avoid repeated no-change updates and repetitive notification volume. Never reactivate hourly radar, publish automatically, or invoke Grok or paid APIs.


## TIKTOK TREND-FIRST DISTRIBUTION GATE — 17 NOV 2026

Purpose: improve TikTok topic selection without allowing platform popularity to override factual accuracy or urgent traveller protection. This gate applies to TikTok editorial recommendations, not the underlying multi-domain research or other channels.

1. **Discover demand before drafting TikTok creative.** Check accessible TikTok Creative Center regional hashtag/topic trends, public TikTok search suggestions and relevant current posts, Google Trends, and independent social/search signals. Record the geography, query, observed metric or rank, URL and EAT observation time. If access or comparable metrics are unavailable, mark TREND_UNVERIFIED. Do not claim TikTok has suppressed a post or that a topic is trending solely from low/high views.
2. **Cross-check the brand's own results.** Where available, inspect Travel Alerts.ke TikTok via connected Metricool: recent post views, publication time, interactions, follower growth, For You and search traffic. Compare equivalent post age and format; do not confuse cumulative views with views earned in a reporting window, or missing values with zero. A strong historical post is a useful editorial signal, not proof of a currently trending topic.
3. **Apply the mandatory evidence and impact gates.** Independently confirm the original incident, event date, current status, exact affected travellers, actionable consequence, official/credible sources and prior coverage. A trending rumour stays MONITOR_ONLY. A recycled incident without a verified material change is rejected, even if its hashtag trends.
4. **Assign TikTok priority:** T1 = verified current traveller impact + independently observed rising audience attention; T2 = verified severe/time-critical P0/P1 traveller impact even without trend evidence; T3 = verified useful travel update without demonstrated trend (normally website/Telegram/X rather than TikTok); WATCH = attention signal awaiting confirmation; REJECT = stale, duplicate, no traveller impact or misleading. Do not suppress T2 safety alerts to optimise reach.
5. **Publish fewer, stronger TikTok candidates.** Recommend at most 1–3 distinct TikTok stories per editorial cycle when eligible; zero is acceptable. Avoid same-event duplicate flyers, identical reposts and repeated advisory summaries. A second post requires a material verified change or explicitly different traveller action, and editorial approval.
6. **Packaging:** A short searchable location/event headline, a factual hook stating the actual traveller consequence, readable 9:16 artwork, one practical action and a credible source. Use relevant specific keywords and restrained hashtags; never invent urgency, closures or 'breaking' labels to chase reach. Research, design and publication remain separate approval stages.
7. **Measure after posting:** Record 24-hour, 7-day and 30-day views, search/For You share where available, interactions, saves, shares and follower conversion where available. Distinguish observed results from hypotheses about algorithm distribution. Compare T1, T2 and T3 performance periodically and refine thresholds using actual data.

**Governing decision:** Trend signals guide TikTok distribution; verified traveller impact and material novelty govern whether the story can be presented as an alert. No paid API calls, hourly radar reactivation or automatic publishing.
