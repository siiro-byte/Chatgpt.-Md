# KA-IN — Kenya Competitor & Industry Source Registry

**Purpose:** Maintain a reliable directory for competitor intelligence, market positioning, product and service benchmarking, industry context and marketing improvement.

**Scope:** Kenya, with Nairobi as the primary operating market unless a research brief specifies otherwise. Compare three categories separately:
1. Furniture and interior-design brands.
2. Interior designers and decorators.
3. Furniture retailers and manufacturers.

## Competitor evidence to capture

For each competitor, collect:

- Official website and active social profiles.
- Business location, showroom footprint and service area.
- Service lines: interior design, space planning, project management, custom furniture, upholstery, restoration, curtains, soft furnishings, décor, office or hospitality work.
- Target segments and apparent positioning (label inference as inference).
- Portfolio quality, project types and evidence of completed work.
- Product categories, visible prices, packages and promotion terms.
- Materials, craftsmanship, manufacturing claims and sourcing.
- Enquiry path: phone, WhatsApp, form, consultation, showroom or quote request.
- Public reviews: rating, review count, recency, recurring themes and response behaviour.
- Content strategy: format, posting frequency, themes, hooks, proof, calls to action and engagement.
- Meta Ad Library activity and publicly visible advertising where accessible.
- Differentiators, customer friction and possible market gaps.

Do not treat follower count, likes or audience size as sales, market share or customer demand.

## Research method

1. Define the decision: positioning, service mix, pricing, content, customer journey, lead generation or competitor threat.
2. Select competitors from all three categories; do not compare a furniture retailer and a full-service design studio as if their business models were identical.
3. Capture dated primary evidence from official websites, social profiles, catalogues, public ads and reviews.
4. Separate observed facts from interpretation and hypotheses.
5. Compare like-for-like services and products; note when pricing or scope is not comparable.
6. Identify gaps KA-IN can credibly serve, not merely trends competitors are using.
7. Convert findings into prioritised actions with expected business impact, effort, evidence and a measurable KPI.
8. Recheck live prices, offers, locations, ads and reviews before relying on them.

## Evidence and reporting labels

- **OBSERVED:** directly visible on a dated official page or public platform.
- **REPORTED:** stated by the business or a third party but not independently verified.
- **INFERENCE:** reasoned interpretation of observed evidence.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** a proposition that needs testing.
- **UNAVAILABLE:** data not publicly available or not accessible through the connected account.

## Cost and access policy

- Default research spend is KES 0.
- Prefer public websites, platform-native free views and existing authorised access.
- No paid scraping, premium data, API credits, subscriptions, upgrades or advertising spend without specific fresh approval.
- Before any paid operation, state provider, purpose, requested data, estimated cost and maximum where known, billing currency and KES equivalent, free alternatives, and wait for approval.
- Do not disconnect existing accounts or change provider plans to obtain data without approval.
