# Stakeholder Update & Memo

## Purpose
Prepare accurate, decision-useful communications for stakeholders. Explain the situation, confirmed facts, implications, decisions, actions, risks and next update without spin or unsupported reassurance.

## When to use
Use for leadership updates, board or investor memos, project status reports, customer notices, incident updates, partner communications and change announcements.

## Required inputs
- Memo purpose and decision/action required
- Intended audience and their interests
- Situation/background and relevant source material
- Confirmed facts, dates, metrics and current status
- Decisions made and decisions still needed
- Actions, owners and deadlines where known
- Risks, dependencies and unresolved issues
- Desired length, tone and distribution sensitivity

## Instructions
1. Identify the audience, purpose and required outcome. Determine whether the memo is to inform, request a decision, secure approval, explain a change or coordinate action.
2. Establish the reporting period and status date. Distinguish event date, decision date and update date where they differ.
3. Organise source material into confirmed facts, reported information, interpretation, assumptions, decisions and unknowns.
4. Lead with the key message or decision required. Do not bury material risks or bad news.
5. Present relevant context and evidence in proportion to the audience's need. Define metrics, baselines and comparisons; do not invent figures or imply causation without support.
6. Explain practical implications for the audience, including what changes, who is affected and what remains unchanged.
7. List actions with owner, due date and status only when those details are supplied or confirmed. Use “owner to be assigned” or a placeholder where necessary.
8. Separate completed actions, work in progress, planned actions and proposed actions. Do not present intentions as completed work.
9. State material risks, dependencies, mitigations and decisions needed. Include the consequence of delay or inaction where supported.
10. Give a realistic next-update point only if known; otherwise state what event or information will trigger the next update.
11. Adjust tone to the situation: transparent, calm and accountable during incidents; concise and decision-oriented for executives; clear and practical for customers or partners.
12. Check confidentiality and distribution. Remove personal or commercially sensitive details that the recipient does not need.

## Expected output
- Memo title, audience and date/status
- Executive summary or key message
- Background and confirmed facts
- Implications and affected parties
- Decisions made and decisions required
- Action register with owners/dates/status where known
- Risks, dependencies and mitigations
- Open questions and next update trigger

## Quality checks
- Key message and required decision are immediately clear
- Facts, interpretations and plans are distinguished
- Dates, figures and status labels are accurate
- Risks and caveats are not hidden
- Actions have clear ownership only when confirmed
- Tone is appropriate and non-misleading
- Confidential information is shared only as necessary
